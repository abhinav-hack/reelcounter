# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** Date of publication

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Publisher Name]** ("we", "us", or "our")
handles information in the **Reel Counter** Android application (the "App").
The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under 13.

## Summary

Reel Counter is designed to work locally on your Android device. In the
current version:

- We do not require an account or collect your name, email address, phone
  number, contacts, precise location, photos, audio, or files.
- We do not operate a server for the App and do not upload your counter
  history or accessibility events.
- We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or in-app
  purchases.
- Counter history and a small amount of app status information are stored
  locally on your device.

## Information processed on your device

The App stores the following information locally:

- Daily scroll counts and the dates associated with those counts.
- A service-connected status value used to show the App's current state.
- A description of the most recent supported-app event, used for troubleshooting
  within the App.

This information remains on your device. It is stored until you clear the
App's data or uninstall the App, unless Android or your device settings
remove it sooner.

## Accessibility Service

To count scrolling activity, the App asks you to enable its Android
Accessibility Service. The service observes accessibility scroll and window
events and uses the package name to identify supported apps. The current
supported-app list includes Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, ShareChat, Moj, and
related package variants.

The service is used only to:

1. detect supported-app scrolling activity;
2. increment the local counter; and
3. show or hide the optional floating counter when a supported app is in the
   foreground.

The App does not use the Accessibility Service to read, save, transmit, or
share the content of messages, posts, videos, passwords, or other on-screen
text. Android may expose accessibility events differently across app and
system versions, so counting may not always be accurate.

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time in Android Settings.
Disabling it stops automatic counting but does not by itself delete locally
stored history.

## Display-over-other-apps permission

The optional floating counter requires Android's "Display over other apps"
permission. The overlay displays the current locally stored count. It is not
used to collect information or record your screen. You can decline this
permission or disable it in Android Settings; the rest of the App can still
be used subject to the features that require it.

## Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share App data with advertisers, data brokers, or
other third parties. Because the current App does not transmit the
information described above, we do not have access to it through a server.

The App may interact with third-party apps such as Instagram, YouTube,
TikTok, ShareChat, and Moj. Those apps are operated by their respective
providers and have their own terms and privacy policies. This Policy does not
cover information those providers collect.

## Security

Local storage is protected by the security controls provided by Android and
your device. No method of storage is completely secure. You are responsible
for protecting access to your device and for using Android's available
security features.

## Your choices and requests

You can:

- disable the Accessibility Service;
- disable the overlay permission;
- clear the App's storage in Android Settings; or
- uninstall the App to remove its locally stored data.

does not send this local data to us, we generally cannot retrieve or delete
it remotely.
